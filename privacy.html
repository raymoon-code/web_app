01 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as when you create an account, contact us, or use features of the App. This may include your name, email address, and usage preferences.

Our applications are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
